# kit-cuts: the plain film

**Shape:** the usual cuts between kit scenes (hook, stat, points, end...), each doing its job and nothing more.
**Choose it when:** the story is a fact and a few numbers, nothing in it can be pictured, or it is news. It is the
right answer more often than agents think: a form must earn its cost.
**Devices:** none (`plain`). **Route:** the kit, `veasy_make`. **News:** yes, it is what `news` is.
**Demands:** nothing new. Pick the director and look by the story; vary scene order from what ran lately.
**Avoid:** forcing a form onto a story with no object, place or number worth a picture.
