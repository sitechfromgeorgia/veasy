# voice-on-record: what was said, as the object

**Shape:** a person's exact words are the picture; an anonymous voice is an abstract figure; a message is shown as a
screen labelled as an example.
**Choose it when:** the story's core is a statement, a quote, a testimony or a message.
**Devices:** `verbatim-quote` (kit: `quote`), `silhouette-witness`, `phone-recreation`.
**Route:** `quote` today; the others wait for the kit, or are composed and held for review.
**News:** `verbatim-quote` yes when the words are in the article; `silhouette-witness` no; `phone-recreation` cond.
**Demands:** the quote is word for word and attributed; a recreation is labelled; no face, no real name on a
recreated screen.
**Avoid:** a paraphrase inside quote marks, blame, a voice for the victim of an incident.
