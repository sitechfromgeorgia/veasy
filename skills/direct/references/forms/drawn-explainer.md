# drawn-explainer: how a thing works, drawn

**Shape:** the object draws itself, or opens into its parts in order; one drawn material carries the whole film.
**Choose it when:** the story is "how does it work": a mechanism, a process, an explainer that is not hard news.
**Devices:** `drawn-reveal` (kit: `draw`, `sign`), `exploded-model` (kit: `draw`, `steps`), `material-film`,
`cut-out-collage`.
**Route:** `draw` and `steps` today; a material or a collage is composed.
**News:** `drawn-reveal` yes; `exploded-model` only for a mechanism, not for an event; `material-film` cond.
**Demands:** parts are named in the order the narration says them; the drawing passes `art.py`; ink and the one
accent only (the accent is a dot, never the colour of text).
**Avoid:** shadows, category colours, a drawing of a person or an incident.
