# evidence-board: items, one by one, until the claim stands

**Shape:** a chain of stated items appears as each is said, or a document's own line is boxed, or events sit on one
clock or line.
**Choose it when:** the story is a proof, a rule, a decision or a timeline: "this is why", "this is when".
**Devices:** `evidence-stack` (kit: `points`, `checklist`), `document-close-up`, `space-time-board` (kit: `rail`,
`timeline`, `calendar`).
**Route:** the kit today; a document close-up of a real law page is composed.
**News:** yes for all three; dates and items are only those the source states, no ordering by cause.
**Demands:** each item is a fact and appears with the word that says it; 2 to 6 items; dates copied.
**Avoid:** an opinion line, a cause the source does not state, a list that is only decoration.
