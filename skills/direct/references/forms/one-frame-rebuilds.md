# one-frame-rebuilds: the same frame, changed

**Shape:** one picture stays; between the narration's two halves it rebuilds itself and what differs is the story
(a valve closes, a table splits into lines, items re-sort into new groups).
**Choose it when:** the story is a before and after, a state that changes, or the same items counted another way.
**Devices:** `state-change` (kit: `change`, `draw`), `data-resort`, `object-becomes-chart` (these two wait for the kit).
**Route:** `change` or `draw` today; the rest is a composed film (`veasy:compose`).
**News:** `state-change` only when the source states both states; the others yes.
**Demands:** both states are facts from the source; the marks keep their identity across the change (the same square
is the same thing); the change lands on the word that says it.
**Avoid:** a change nobody stated, a computed difference (N2).
