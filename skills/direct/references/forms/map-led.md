# map-led: where it happened is the film

**Shape:** a plain map, one area filled or one route drawn; the camera flies to the place or follows the line.
**Choose it when:** the story is a place, a spread or a journey: a district, a region, a route from A to B.
**Devices:** `clean-map`, `map-flight`, `route-arc` (kit: `where`, `route`), `before-after-wipe`, `parallax`.
**Route:** the kit (`where`, `route`) today; a dated before and after is composed.
**News:** yes. Georgia is its 12 regions of Georgian law; Tbilisi is points, never district shapes; for crime the map
stops at the region (`geo.PRIVATE`).
**Demands:** an `area` from `options --section scenes`; the credit under the map stays on screen.
**Avoid:** a map for a story with no place, a district shape, a pin on a private address.
