# number-body: the number has a size you can see

**Shape:** the number is shown as marks you can count, as a thing beside it, or alone on an empty canvas.
**Choose it when:** one figure is the story and a bare "6 400" will slide past: a countable 2 to 30, a ratio too large
for one screen, a size you can compare with something public and constant.
**Devices:** `unit-array` (kit: `stat`, `share`), `yardstick` (kit: `compare`, `versus`, `bars`), `too-tall`,
`pour-or-pile`, `empty-hold` (kit: `stat`).
**Route:** the kit for the first, second and last; `figures` when 2 to 4 numbers share one beat; the rest composed.
**News:** `unit-array`, `too-tall`, `empty-hold` yes; `yardstick`, `pour-or-pile` only with a stated unit.
**Demands:** the number is copied from the source, never summed or rounded; the comparison thing is public and
constant; one unit; the number is in the narration too.
**Avoid:** a size comparison that is a guess, a pie, a share the article does not state.
