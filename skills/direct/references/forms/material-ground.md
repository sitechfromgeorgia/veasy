# material-ground: the film stands on one material

**Shape:** a slow lit surface (stone, paper, wood, cloth, rust) behind solid cards, the same material in every plate scene.
**Choose it when:** the story is an idea, a system or a process, and a material belongs to its subject: paper for a rule,
stone for something that lasts, rust for what is neglected.
**Devices:** `material-film`, `treated-picture` (kit: `plate`).
**Route:** the kit: a `plate` scene, and the shelf picks a surface once `engine plate shelf` has baked it.
**News:** no, nor crime, incidents or deaths: a plate is refused there.
**Demands:** the words stay on their card; a material a model made carries the pack's label by itself.
**Avoid:** a surface that says nothing about the story; a real place: a plate is made, never a picture of somewhere.
