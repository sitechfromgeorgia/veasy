# one-take: the camera moves, the cuts vanish

**Shape:** one world on screen; the camera travels through its steps (a street, a document, a scale from a city to a
district). No hard cut, a move carries each beat.
**Choose it when:** the story is a sequence in one place or on one thing: how a process runs, a scale that narrows.
**Devices:** `one-take-world`, `scale-steps` (kit: `where`), `rostrum-move`, `parallax`.
**Route:** composed (`veasy:compose`); a short move over one picture can be a plate (`plates/README.md`).
**News:** yes, with a real place or a drawn world, never a picture of a real place made by a model.
**Demands:** one move per beat, each ending on the fact it carries; text stays in the DOM; the flash check passes.
**Avoid:** a move with no reason (reveal, connect or stop: nothing else), shaking, fast zoom loops.
