# Crayon Picture Book (`crayon-book`)

> Charming children's illustrated storybook drawn live in wax crayon on textured cotton paper with hand-drawn Georgian lettering.

## 1. At-a-Glance Signature
- Textured warm cream cotton paper background (`#FBF8EE`) with visible organic paper tooth.
- Heavy wax crayon linework featuring characteristic waxy skips, edge grit, and vivid crayon pigments.
- Watercolor resist washes filling background areas behind waxy crayon outlines.
- Playful, authentic child-logic perspective and joyful character proportions.

## 2. Brand & Program Rules (Strict)
- **Palette: Own & Fonts: Own**: Warm paper ground (`#FBF8EE`), primary crayon wax pigments (cobalt blue `#2563EB`, poppy red `#DC2626`, sun yellow `#F59E0B`, forest green `#16A34A`, charcoal crayon `#1F2937`).
- **Brand Law Invariant**: **Strictly forbidden for news programs (`news`)**. Allowed only for storytelling and children's literature programs, and **only when explicitly listed in `brand.json` under `"styles"`**.

## 3. Vertical (1080×1920) Geometry & Safe Zones
- Illustrated frame: $x \in [80, 1000]$, $y \in [240, 1360]$.
- Crayon characters and scenes occupy mid-frame.
- Story captions sit in the lower third: $y \in [1380, 1552]$ on a soft paper strip.
- Right margin clear zone: $x \le 940$ below $y = 1100$.

## 4. Georgian Mkhedruli Typography
- Organic hand-lettered Georgian script with gentle round curves and playful letter heights.
- Story narrative text rendered in friendly rounded font (`FiraGO Medium` or bespoke hand-lettering).
- Warm dark crayon ink (`#1F2937`), avoiding harsh digital black.

## 5. Motion & Animation Rules
- Hand-drawn line boil on twos (12 fps or 15 fps) giving living paper vibration.
- Crayon strokes draw out along paths with natural wax resistance and pressure speed variation.
- Transitions: turning of a textured picture-book paper page or playful crayon scribble wipe.

## 6. Sound Craft
- Gentle, acoustic music bed (kalimba, nylon acoustic guitar, toy piano, or soft flute).
- Constant volume without ducking or pumping.
- SFX: soft paper page turn whoosh (`whoosh`), warm wooden block pop (`pop`), soft crayon friction.
