# Blueprint (`blueprint`)

> Orthographic architectural or engineering blueprint in white hairlines on Prussian cyanotype blue paper with title blocks and live dimensions.

## 1. At-a-Glance Signature
- Deep Prussian blue cyanotype ground (`#18336B`) with crisp chalk-white technical linework (`#E7F0F9`).
- Orthographic projection: plan, elevation, section views, centre lines (dash-dot), and 45° cross-hatching.
- Architectural title block at bottom right specifying revision number, scale, date, and engineer stamp.
- Dimension chains with dimension tick marks and live numeric callouts.

## 2. Brand & Program Rules (Strict)
- **Palette: Own**: Brings its own cyanotype blue ground (`#18336B`), white ink (`#E7F0F9`), and cyan accent (`#38BDF8`).
- **Brand Law Invariant**: **Forbidden for news programs (`news`)**. Allowed only for explainers, technical blog posts, or stories, and **only when explicitly listed in `brand.json` under `"styles"`** (e.g. SiTech).

## 3. Vertical (1080×1920) Geometry & Safe Zones
- Blueprint grid frame: $x \in [54, 1026]$, $y \in [180, 1380]$.
- Title block: occupies $x \in [500, 940]$, $y \in [1220, 1360]$ within the lower safe zone.
- Caption boundary: Captions remain in lower safe band $y \in [1380, 1552]$.
- TikTok rail clearance: $x \le 940$ below $y = 1100$.

## 4. Georgian Mkhedruli Typography
- Technical drawing lettering: clean `FiraGO 500` or `IBM Plex Mono 400` with open letter spacing.
- Architectural title block uses all-caps Latin or bold Mkhedruli with technical project identifiers (e.g. `პროექტი #842-A`).
- Dimension markers use Georgian technical symbols: „მმ“, „სმ“, „მ“, „კმ“.

## 5. Motion & Animation Rules
- Blueprint drafting effect: lines reveal progressively via vector stroke-dashoffset over 25 frames.
- Center lines and dimension lines drop in with precision compass-like snaps.
- Stamp mark: engineering approval stamp slams down with high opacity.
- Transitions: blueprint sliding roll or vertical drafting ruler wipe.

## 6. Sound Craft
- Focused mechanical drafting ambiance or minimalist technical loop.
- Constant volume without ducking or pumping.
- SFX: mechanical drafting compass click (`click`), precise drafting pen tick (`tick`).
