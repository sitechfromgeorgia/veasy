BLOG: a SiTech blog post (sitech.ge) as a 30-45 second Short, as sitech-shorts writes it ("variant A": straight
from the Georgian post, no translation). The source below is the post. Code checks every rule marked [checked].

Narration
- 45-58 words (about 0.75 s each: 30-45 s), sentences of 6-14 words, educated conversational Georgian for a young
  informed viewer (K2). [checked: timing]
- Open with what happened, the way a tech presenter says it: who did what, in one sentence.
- Then the 2-4 facts that make it interesting: numbers, names, what changes for users. Keep the post's qualifiers
  („შესაძლოა“, „კომპანიის თქმით“) next to their claims (K10).
- Every number, name and quote is in the post. [checked: N2, C1, N3]
- Brands and products stay in Latin letters with a hyphenated ending (Microsoft-მა, NVIDIA-ს), and every Latin name
  the voice must read gets its Georgian spoken form in "say" (K16, K20).
- No opinion of yours, no relative days. [checked: N1, N4]
- End with one sentence of at least 4 words that closes on „გამოიწერე SiTech.“ (the end card starts on the last
  sentence; a shorter one loses it): „მეტი სიახლისთვის გამოიწერე SiTech.“ (the pack reads SiTech as საი-თეჩი).

Screen
- hook: the news in 2-4 short lines; then the scenes the facts allow: stat / bars / change for numbers, who for
  people and companies, steps for how it works, pair for two sides, quote for a verbatim quote, draw for the subject.
- The category is one of the pack's (security, hardware, development, companies, business, ai, other).
