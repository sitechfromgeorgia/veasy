EXPLAINER: one document explained in 30-45 seconds.
The source below is ONE document (a web page, a report, a PDF, an announcement). The video says what it says, in
its own numbers, names and words, so a viewer gets the point without reading it. Code checks every rule marked
[checked].

Narration
- 45-58 words (about 0.75 s each: 30-45 s), sentences of 6-14 words, one idea each, active voice. [checked: timing]
- The first sentence says what the document is and its main point: „ახალი კვლევის თანახმად, ...“, „X-ის წესები
  ამბობს, რომ ...“.
- Then the 2-4 facts that matter most, in the document's order. Attribute claims to the document or its author
  („ანგარიშის მიხედვით“, „კომპანიის თქმით“): the video reports, it doesn't endorse.
- Every number is the document's own, written as it writes it; no sums, differences or rounding of yours. [checked: N2]
- Every name (a person, a company, a product, a body) is in the document. [checked: C1]
- A quote is verbatim, in „…“, with who said it. [checked: N3]
- No opinion or evaluative words of yours (a word the document itself uses is fine). [checked: N1]
- No relative days: name the date the document gives, or leave it out. [checked: N4]
- End with the brand's call to action in one sentence of at least 4 words (the end card starts on the last sentence;
  a shorter one loses it): tbilisi.today „მეტი განმარტებისთვის გამოიწერე ჩვენი არხი.“, SiTech „მეტი სიახლისთვის
  გამოიწერე SiTech.“

Screen
- hook: what the document is about in 2-4 short lines.
- A number → stat (one), bars (2-5 of one kind), trend (3-8 over time) or change (then and now).
- A procedure → steps; two sides → pair; separate points → points; a person or body → who; a place → where.
- draw: one drawing of the subject when nothing else fits.
- No sources scene: this is one document, the footer names it.
