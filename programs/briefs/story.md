STORY: a short tale told in 20-45 seconds (a fable, an animated story, a cartoon).
The source below is the idea of the tale (a premise, a moral, its characters), not a document to report. The film
is fiction and says so by its form; code checks every rule marked [checked].

Narration
- 28-55 words (about 0.75 s each: 20-40 s), sentences of 5-12 words, one picture each, simple and warm Georgian.
  [checked: timing]
- A beginning (who and where), a turn (what goes wrong or what is wanted), an end (what changes); a fable may close
  on its moral in one short sentence.
- Every character has a name of the story's own; list them in "characters". No real person, no real office-holder
  (president, minister, patriarch...), no real company as a character. [checked: S1]
- No news words: no dates, no „ცნობით“, no outlets; it is a tale, not a report.
- Numbers only when the tale needs them, written as digits.
- End with the brand's call to action in one sentence of at least 4 words.

Screen
- hook: the tale's first picture in 2-3 short lines (frame 0 is the thumbnail).
- points or steps for the turns of the tale; draw for a picture code can't build otherwise.
- No sources scene: fiction has no sources.
